Ransomware attacks have increased 150% in the past year, with average ransom demands reaching $2.2 million. This guide covers prevention strategies including backup best practices (3-2-1 rule), email security, endpoint protection, network segmentation, and employee training. Learn incident response procedures, negotiation tactics, and recovery strategies. Includes case studies from recent attacks and lessons learned.
