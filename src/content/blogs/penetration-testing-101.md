Penetration testing simulates real-world attacks to identify security weaknesses. This beginner-friendly guide covers the penetration testing lifecycle: reconnaissance, scanning, exploitation, and reporting. Learn essential tools like Nmap, Metasploit, Burp Suite, and Wireshark. Understand OWASP Top 10 vulnerabilities, SQL injection, XSS, CSRF, and authentication bypasses. Includes lab setup instructions, legal considerations, and career paths in cybersecurity.
