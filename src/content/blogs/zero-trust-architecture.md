Zero Trust Architecture operates on the principle of 'never trust, always verify.' Unlike traditional security models that trust everything inside the network perimeter, Zero Trust assumes breaches are inevitable and verifies every access request.

## Key Components

### Identity Verification
Every user and device must be authenticated and authorized before accessing resources.

### Micro-segmentation
Network is divided into small zones to maintain separate access for separate parts of the network.

### Least-Privilege Access
Users are given the minimum level of access necessary to perform their job functions.

### Continuous Monitoring
All network activity is continuously monitored and analyzed for potential threats.

## Implementation Benefits

Organizations implementing Zero Trust have seen up to 70% reduction in security incidents. The model provides better visibility, reduces attack surface, and improves compliance posture.

## Getting Started

Start with identity and access management (IAM), implement multi-factor authentication (MFA), and gradually segment your network based on data sensitivity and business requirements.
