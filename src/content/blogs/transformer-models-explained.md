Transformers have revolutionized AI since the 'Attention Is All You Need' paper. Unlike RNNs, transformers process entire sequences simultaneously using self-attention mechanisms. We'll break down the architecture: multi-head attention, positional encodings, feed-forward networks, and residual connections. Learn how BERT, GPT, and T5 build on this foundation. Includes PyTorch implementation examples and training tips.
