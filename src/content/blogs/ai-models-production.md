Moving AI models to production requires more than just good accuracy. This guide covers MLOps best practices including model versioning with DVC, containerization with Docker, orchestration with Kubernetes, monitoring model drift, A/B testing strategies, and implementing CI/CD pipelines for ML. Learn how companies like Netflix and Spotify deploy hundreds of models efficiently.
