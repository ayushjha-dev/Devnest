Blockchain technology extends far beyond Bitcoin and Ethereum. This guide explores enterprise use cases: supply chain transparency with Walmart's Food Trust, healthcare record management with MedRec, secure voting systems in Estonia, and self-sovereign identity with decentralized identifiers (DIDs). Learn about permissioned blockchains (Hyperledger Fabric), smart contract development with Solidity, consensus mechanisms, and when blockchain is (and isn't) the right solution. Includes cost-benefit analysis and implementation roadmaps.
